Tag a link to a video

Adds UTM tags so Google Analytics can tell which video sent the click. See the caveat below for Kit and ThriveCart pages.

Google Analytics reads UTM tags only on pages where your GA tag runs, such as your Squarespace site. Kit and ThriveCart pages sit on their own domains. For those, the dependable route is one Kit form or tag per video call-to-action, and a ThriveCart link or coupon per video. Check what your plan supports before relying on UTM pass-through.

  2. When the chat reschedules or adds scripts, ask it to reply with the same table.
  3. Paste that table back into the box and press Preview import, then Apply. Dates and columns update, and unknown videos arrive as new cards.

Website data from Google Analytics

Free, one-time, about 15 minutes. A small Google script copies your daily Analytics numbers into a Google Sheet you own, and this page reads the Sheet.

  1. Create a new Google Sheet. Open Extensions, then Apps Script.
  2. Press Copy script below and paste it in. Add the "Google Analytics Data API" under Services.
  3. Set your GA4 property ID and your waitlist and blog paths at the top of the script.
  4. Run updateGa4 once, approve the permissions, then run installDailyTrigger.
  5. Paste the Sheet's link into the Website card on the Funnel tab.

Google Analytics does not report search impressions. That comes from Google Search Console and can use the same Sheet approach later.
